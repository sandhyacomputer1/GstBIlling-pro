1. Introduction

Welcome to GSTBilling Pro ("we", "our", "us", "the App"), developed and maintained by Sandhya SoftTech Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our Android application for GST billing, invoicing, and business management.

By downloading, installing, or using GSTBilling Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about the data we handle.

2. Information We Collect

To provide GST billing and invoicing services, GSTBilling Pro collects the following categories of information:

  • Business information provided during registration and setup
  • Customer details entered by you for invoicing purposes
  • Product and inventory information
  • Invoice and GST billing records
  • Authentication details (mobile number and PIN)
  • Basic device and app usage information for troubleshooting

3. Business Information

When you set up your business profile, we collect the following details to generate accurate GST invoices and reports:

GSTIN and Email Address are optional fields — you can use core app features without providing them, though certain GST-compliant invoice formats may require a valid GSTIN.

4. Customer Information

GSTBilling Pro allows you to save your customers' details — such as name, contact number, address, and GSTIN — to generate invoices quickly. This information is entered and managed entirely by you (the business owner) and is used solely for billing and record-keeping within your business account.

5. Product Information

The App stores details of products and services you sell, including product name, description, price, HSN/SAC code, GST rate, and stock quantity. This data helps generate accurate, GST-compliant invoices and track inventory.

6. Invoice Information

Every invoice generated through the App — including invoice number, date, itemized billing details, tax breakdown, and payment status — is securely stored so you can retrieve, edit, print, or share it as a PDF at any time.

7. GST Billing Data

GST-specific data such as CGST, SGST, IGST calculations, tax slabs, and GSTIN details of both business and customers are processed within the App to help you remain GST-compliant. This data is used exclusively for invoice generation and reporting purposes and is not used for any unrelated purpose.

8. Authentication

GSTBilling Pro uses secure authentication methods to protect access to your business data:

Mobile Number Login
PIN Login

Your Mobile Number is used as the primary identifier for authentication, and your PIN adds an extra layer of security for quick, secure access to your account.

9. Firebase Usage

GSTBilling Pro uses Google Firebase, a secure cloud platform, to store and sync your business data reliably:

  • Firebase Realtime Database — securely stores business, customer, product, and invoice data in the cloud
  • Firebase Authentication (if enabled) — helps verify user identity securely
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (if enabled) — used to deliver app notifications and updates

Firebase is operated by Google LLC and complies with industry-standard security practices. Your data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit.

10. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your business profile
  • To generate GST-compliant invoices and bills
  • To authenticate and secure your account
  • To sync your data securely across sessions
  • To provide customer support and resolve issues
  • To improve app performance and reliability

11. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud infrastructure) and, where applicable, locally on your device for offline access. We do not store your data on unsecured or third-party servers outside of Google's trusted infrastructure.

12. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encrypted data transmission, secure Firebase access rules, and restricted internal access.

Security Notice

All data transmitted between the App and Firebase is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your business data is restricted to your authenticated account only.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to any third party. Your data is used solely to provide and improve the App's services. We may share limited data only with:

  • Google Firebase — for secure cloud storage and authentication
  • Legal authorities — only when required by applicable law

Data Protection Commitment

We never sell your personal information. Your business and customer data remain private and are used only to power your GST billing experience within the App.

14. Third Party Services

GSTBilling Pro relies on trusted third-party services to operate effectively:

  • Google Firebase — cloud database, authentication, and messaging
  • Google Play Services — core Android functionality

These providers have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information, and we encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy for additional details.

15. Data Retention

We retain your business, customer, product, and invoice data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide you with the App's services. Data is retained to allow you to access historical invoices and reports at any time.

16. User Rights

As a user of GSTBilling Pro, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal and business data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data or account
  • Withdraw consent for optional data collection (e.g., email, GSTIN)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below.

17. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your GSTBilling Pro account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team at support@sandhyasofttech.com. Once verified, your account data will be removed from our active systems in accordance with our data retention policy, except where retention is required to comply with legal or regulatory obligations (such as GST record-keeping laws).

18. Children's Privacy

GSTBilling Pro is a business tool intended for use by adults managing GST billing operations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

19. Cookies and Analytics

GSTBilling Pro, as a native Android application, does not use browser cookies. We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics (such as crash reporting and app performance metrics) to help us understand app stability and improve the user experience. This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.

20. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my business data safe with GSTBilling Pro?
Yes. Your data is stored on Google Firebase with encrypted transmission and restricted access controls limited to your authenticated account.
Do I have to provide my GSTIN or Email Address?
No, both GSTIN and Email Address are optional. You can use most features of the App without providing them, though a GSTIN may be needed for certain GST-compliant invoice formats.
How do I delete my account and data?
Simply email us at support@sandhyasofttech.com with your account deletion request. We will verify your identity and remove your data in accordance with our data retention policy.
Does GSTBilling Pro sell my data to advertisers?
No. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to any third party, including advertisers.
Can I correct incorrect information in my profile?
Yes, you can update most information directly within the App. For assistance, you may also contact our support team.
What happens to my data after account deletion?
Your data is removed from our active systems according to company policy, except where retention is legally required, such as for GST record-keeping obligations.
