Step‑by‑Step Guide

How to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps inside the GSTBilling Pro app to permanently delete your account and business data.

  1. 1

    Open GSTBilling Pro

    Launch the GSTBilling Pro app on your Android device and sign in to your account.

  2. 2

    Go to Settings

    Navigate to the Settings menu from the app's main navigation panel.

  3. 3

    Tap Delete Account

    Locate and select the "Delete Account" option under account management settings.

  4. 4

    Read the Confirmation Message

    Carefully review the confirmation dialog explaining what will happen once your account is deleted.

  5. 5

    Enter Your PIN

    For your security, enter your account PIN to verify and authorize the deletion request.

  6. 6

    Tap Delete Account

    Confirm the action by tapping the final "Delete Account" button to submit your request.

  7. Account & Business Data Permanently Deleted

    Your account and all associated business data are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my account after deletion?

No. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered or restored under any circumstances. Please make sure you want to proceed before confirming.

Will my invoices be deleted?

Yes. All invoices, along with your customers, products, purchase records, sales records and reports, are permanently deleted along with your account.

How long does deletion take?

Account deletion is processed immediately after confirmation. Your account and business data are permanently removed from our systems right away.

Do I need to contact support to delete my account?

No. You can delete your account entirely by yourself directly within the GSTBilling Pro app. Contacting support is optional and only needed if you face issues.

Is my Firebase data deleted too?

Yes. Your associated Firebase account data is permanently deleted as part of the account deletion process.
